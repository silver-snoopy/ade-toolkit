ADE vs. the Field — Re-benchmark (2026-06)

A maximum-rigor re-run of the agentic-SDLC field study after ADE closed its five gaps (G1–G5). It re-scores ADE against an expanded 17-system field on twelve dev-core dimensions, and then asks the headline question: what capabilities of a standards-grade SDLC does the entire field still skip?

Bottom line

ADE is now a top-tier system on rigor (research, blind verification, hook-enforced TDD) and has closed 4 of 5 gaps, but the gap-closure work quietly removed live verification (new G6 regression) and G2/hooks remains only partial. The decisive strategic move is NOT chasing peers on the dimensions everyone already plays — it is being the first to treat the standard-SDLC tail the whole field misses (threat modeling, observability/runbooks, incident->learning, privacy, supply-chain SBOM) as enforced, scaffolded pipeline steps. Two areas (incident-postmortem loop, privacy/PII) are covered by ZERO of 17 systems and align directly with ADE's existing compound-loop and spec-verification strengths, making them leapfrog bets rather than catch-up work.

Post-gap-closure, ADE has moved from a research-and-spec specialist into a credible end-to-end contender, and it now LEADS the 16-system field on the hardest-to-fake quality dimensions: research rigor (the only iterative-retrieval + grill-with-docs + factor+revise CoVe trifecta in the set), blind generator!=verifier isolation (spec-verifier is structurally forbidden the spec text), hook-enforced TDD author separation (the ONLY system with a live EXIT=2 block-mixed-commit gate), durable spec+glossary+ADR artifacts, phase-masking blast routing with a deterministic backstop, a closed compound loop where a per-finding-class frequency metric actually tunes the downstream review gate, and stack-agnosticism by construction. Four of the five original gaps (G1 TDD author-separation, G3 compound loop, G4 blast routing, G5 stack-agnostic) are fully CLOSED and several are now leader-class. G2 (deterministic hooks) is genuinely PARTIAL: ADE ships three real off-loop content hooks — more than the scout claimed — but the original ambition (PostToolUse, AST checks, silent-catch/file-size/format hooks) is unmet, the dimension lands at strong-not-leader, and the escalation hook no-ops off ade/* branches. The post-modernization picture also surfaced a REGRESSION the gap-closure work introduced: live verification was explicitly REMOVED from the pipeline and replaced with a test-coverage gate, dropping ADE from any live-evidence story to test-coverage-only (a new G6-candidate gap), and ADE remains absent on cross-harness portability by design. But the headline is not ADE's internal scorecard — it is that the ENTIRE 16-system field, ADE included, systematically skips the operational, security-design, and compliance tail of a standards-grade SDLC. The single biggest opportunity is not catching peers on liveVerify or hooks; it is leading the field into the standard-SDLC areas almost nobody covers.

00Method & corpus

This study re-runs the original 2026-06-19 analysis at higher effort. ADE was re-scored by reading its actual generated tree (ade init in a scratch repo + the template sources), not its claims. The field was expanded from 9 to 17 systems by mining a supplied meta-source (shanraisshan/claude-code-best-practice) for additional SDLC methodologies. A multi-agent workflow then ran 15 primary-source readers (one per system, reading real agent/skill/command/hook files via authenticated gh), each immediately paired with an adversarial verifier instructed to refute inflated ratings; 8 research agents covered cross-cutting concepts and the standard-SDLC yardstick (ISO/IEC 12207, SWEBOK, NIST SSDF, DORA, OWASP SAMM, Google SRE, SLSA, WCAG); and every uncovered-area claim was independently refuted on two axes ("is it really standard?" and "is it really uncovered?"). 56 agents total.

04Capabilities · best practices · patterns

The analysis, organized around the four focus dimensions. ADE is now leader-class on 7 of 12 dev-core dimensions.

Capabilities — where ADE now stands in the field

ADE: leader on 7/12; the research+blind-verification stack is the field's most rigorous

Against the 2025-2026 capabilities frontier (orchestrator-worker multi-agent, spec-as-executable-artifact, deterministic mechanical gates, compound learning loops, context engineering), ADE is now a full-spectrum participant rather than a research-only tool. It is leader-class on 7 of 12 dimensions: researchPhase, blindCoveVerification, tddAuthorSeparation, durableSpec, blastRouting, compoundLoop, and stackAgnostic. It is strong (not leader) on deterministicHooks, reviewFan, and modelTiering. It is behind on liveVerify (regressed to partial) and absent on crossHarness. The standout capability is the research+verification stack: no other surveyed system combines iterative-retrieval scouts, vendored grill-with-docs, and factor+revise Chain-of-Verification with a structurally spec-blind verifier — this is the single most rigorous recon-and-verify pipeline in the set, and it directly operationalizes the field consensus that generator!=verifier (Huang et al. ICLR 2024).

Best practices — convergent, evidence-backed disciplines

ADE: strong adherence; the one violated best-practice is live end-to-end verification (the G6 regression)

On the evidence-backed best-practice axis, ADE adheres tightly to the load-bearing ones. Generator!=verifier: ADE's spec-verifier never sees the spec — the cleanest hard isolation in the field. Author-separated TDD (Anthropic's explicit recommendation): ADE is the ONLY system that hook-enforces it (block-mixed-commit, live EXIT=2). Durable spec as source of truth / review-the-plan-not-the-code (HumanLayer CRISPY, Anthropic): ADE writes a permanent spec + glossary + ADR chain that survives /clear. ~2-reviewer optimum: ADE's review fan (4-5 lenses) is reasonable though single-model. Context engineering / subagent isolation: scouts and verifiers run in fresh windows returning condensed findings. The ONE best-practice ADE now VIOLATES post-modernization is explore-then-plan-then-code with a live end-to-end verification step that PROVES the feature works (Anthropic best practices explicitly: specs should 'end with an end-to-end verification step that proves the feature works'). ADE replaced per-criterion live proof with a test-coverage gate — adhering to the letter of TDD exit criteria but abandoning the 'fresh evidence the running system works' discipline that case/gstack/gsd/HumanLayer enforce.

Patterns — recurring architecture & process choices

ADE: inside all dominant patterns; conscious non-adoption of cross-model review and cross-harness

ADE sits firmly inside the dominant patterns and makes defensible trade-offs. Orchestrator + bounded workers: ADE uses Opus-orchestrator/Sonnet-Haiku-workers with explicit task contracts (the four-part contract Anthropic flags as load-bearing). Blast-radius routing (Microsoft CAF's primary mandate for going multi-agent): ADE's 0d tier classifier masks phases by blast radius with a deterministic forced-escalation backstop — a true phase-masking router, matching gstack/ECC and ahead of the many router-as-informational systems. Compound loop (Every/Shipper, the canonical plan->work->review->compound): ADE closes it bidirectionally — the review-calibration frequency metric tunes the Phase-6 gate and Phase-1 scouts read learnings back. Deterministic hooks (the standard off-model guardrail, PreToolUse the only proactive-deny event): ADE has a real but narrow layer — three content hooks across PreToolUse(Bash)+pre-commit, but no PostToolUse, no AST, and the escalation hook is branch-scoped to ade/*, not repo-wide. ADE deliberately does NOT adopt cross-model adversarial review (its fan is all-Sonnet, where gstack/compound-engineering fan across model families for decorrelated blind spots) nor cross-harness portability — both conscious scope choices, not oversights.

Uncovered standard-SDLC areas — the headline

ADE: ADE shares the field-wide blind spot; this is the headline opportunity, not a per-system weakness

The most important finding is field-wide, not ADE-specific: across 17 systems, the whole field clusters at the code/PR boundary and systematically skips the operational, security-design, and compliance tail that ISO/IEC 12207, SWEBOK, NIST SSDF, Google SRE, DORA, OWASP SAMM, SLSA, and WCAG all treat as standard. Of 16 confirmed-standard areas, the field's coverage is near-zero on the ones that live in production or in legal/governance: incident->learning postmortem loops (0 of 17 ingest a real production postmortem), data/privacy compliance / PII handling (0 of 17), observability/runbooks/SLOs as a build deliverable (1 of 17 — only agent-skills), accessibility as a HARD gate (1 of 17 enforces, the rest are informational), and threat modeling as a DESIGN-time STRIDE gate distinct from code-level review (3 of 17). Better-covered tail areas — data-migration safety, API/contract versioning — are still mostly static review, with NOBODY running executable consumer-driven contract tests or up->down->up reversibility proofs. The structural reason is consistent: agentic tools optimize the pre-merge inner loop (diff in, findings out) and these areas have no diff signal, require runtime/cloud/ops context outside the sandbox, or are legal/governance overlays. ADE specifically: only code-level security review (no design-time threat model), no observability/runbook deliverable, no privacy/PII step, no SBOM/license gate, no NFR/capacity taxonomy category, no release-safety (rollback/canary/flag) gate. This tail — not liveVerify parity or another hook — is the next frontier.

The structural reason

Agentic tools optimize the pre-merge inner loop — diff in, findings out. The uncovered tail has no diff signal (privacy, observability targets), requires runtime/cloud/ops context outside the sandbox (canary, load, incidents), or is a legal/governance overlay (GDPR, SBOM mandates). The field clusters where the feedback is cheap and skips where a standards-grade SDLC actually lives in production.

08The next frontier for ADE

The leapfrog thesis

The next frontier is not catching peers on live verification or hook count — it is being the first system to treat the OPERATIONAL, SECURITY-DESIGN, and COMPLIANCE tail of a standards-grade SDLC as enforced, scaffolded pipeline steps. The entire 16-system field clusters at the code/PR boundary and skips what ISO/IEC 12207, NIST SSDF, Google SRE, OWASP SAMM, SLSA, and GDPR all treat as standard: 0 of 17 systems ingest a real production postmortem into a learning loop, 0 of 17 handle data/privacy (PII), only 1 of 17 ships observability/runbooks as a build deliverable, only 1 of 17 enforces accessibility as a hard gate, and only 3 of 17 do design-time STRIDE threat modeling. Two of these — the incident->learning loop and privacy/PII — are covered by NOBODY and map directly onto ADE's existing strengths (its closed compound loop already reads docs/learnings/ back; its spec-verifier already does blind falsifiable checking). Leading into this tail, rather than chasing the dimensions every system already plays, is how ADE leapfrogs the field instead of merely matching it.
